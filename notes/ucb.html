01Investment thesis

We initiate on UCB with a HOLD rating and a target price of €298, 3.6% above the 17 November close of €287.60. On 22 October UCB raised its 2026 guidance for the second time this year. Revenue is now expected to grow in the low twenties at constant currency and adjusted EBITDA in the low-to-mid forties. The shares rose 21.6% that day and are up 35% since. We think the upgrade is real, but at 21x our 2026 earnings the price already reflects it.

Three reasons

  • The upgrade is large and comes from demand. US demand for Bimzelx, better net prices and a steadier Cimzia drove the change. Market reports put the new guidance at about €9.1-9.3bn of revenue and €3.5-3.6bn of adjusted EBITDA, against a consensus of about €8.7bn and €3.0bn before the release.
  • The price has moved further than the numbers. The implied 2026 EBITDA is about 17% above the consensus before the release, but the shares are up 35% since 21 October. They trade at 21.3x 2026E earnings against a peer median of 18.6x. Our DCF gives €303 per share, close to the price.
  • Growth rests on one drug. Bimzelx made 37% of net sales in the first half of 2026 and most of the growth. Cimzia faces biosimilars after 2030 and Bimzelx loses protection in the next decade. We model both, and that caps our value.
What would make us wrong

If Bimzelx beats the company's own peak-sales goal of at least €7bn and the pipeline (galvokimig, cizutamig) delivers, our bull case gives €446. On the other side, our discount rate is low because UCB's measured beta is only 0.11: at a WACC of 7.4% the base case falls to €253, which would mean a Reduce rating.

02The surprise: a second upgrade, twice as large

In July UCB had already raised its guidance, but the shares fell more than 8% because the implied second-half profit was below forecasts. On 22 October the company issued a new update outside its normal calendar. Revenue growth guidance moved from the low-to-mid teens to the low twenties, and adjusted EBITDA growth from mid-teens to low twenties to the low-to-mid forties, both at constant currency. The guidance excludes the planned sale of a US priority review voucher, which could add a few more points.

The shares closed at €259.10 on 22 October, up 21.64% from €213.00, and kept rising. By 9 November they reached a record close of €298.80, and they closed at €287.60 on 17 November, 35% above the pre-release price. The CFO said the group is turning revenue growth into "greater operating leverage": EBITDA now grows about twice as fast as sales.

03History and estimates

UCB's history has two parts. From 2019 to 2023 revenue stalled near €5-6bn as Cimzia, Vimpat and Keppra matured and Vimpat lost US exclusivity in 2022. The margin fell from 29% to 23%. From 2024 five launches (Bimzelx, Fintepla, Rystiggo, Zilbrysq and Evenity) took over: revenue rose 17% in 2024 and 26% in 2025 to €7.74bn, and the adjusted EBITDA margin reached 34%. 2025 also includes €315m from selling older brands.

Key investments and events

  • 2020: buys Ra Pharma (Zilbrysq) for about $2.1bn.
  • 2022: buys Zogenix (Fintepla) for about €1.2bn in cash; Vimpat loses US exclusivity.
  • 2023-2024: Bimzelx approved in the US, then in hidradenitis suppurativa (HS).
  • 2025: revenue €7.7bn; sells non-core brands for €315m; ends the year with no net debt.
  • 2026: buys Candid Therapeutics for $2.0bn upfront plus up to $0.2bn, and Neurona Therapeutics; starts a US biologics campus in Georgia, its largest manufacturing project.
  • Workforce: 10,117 people at end-2025, of which 3,706 in Belgium and 1,885 in the US.

Our 2026 estimate of €9.20bn revenue and €3.55bn adjusted EBITDA sits inside the range implied by the new guidance. For 2027 we expect 12% growth: Bimzelx keeps gaining share, while Briviact loses about 80% of US sales and half of European sales within a year of generic entry. Our 2026 core EPS of €13.50 is 2% above the consensus of €13.19.

Free cash flow doubled to €2.0bn in 2025 and UCB ended the year with a small net cash position. The Candid deal, paid in the first half of 2026, took net debt back to €2.82bn, or 0.9x EBITDA. That is low, and second-half cash flow should cut it further.

UCB trades at a premium to the large pharma groups and at a discount to the two fastest growers, argenx and Lilly. That fits: its sales growth of about 20% is between the two groups.

Applying the peer median of 14.6x to UCB's trailing EBITDA of €3.14bn gives €226 per share; the upper quartile of 23.4x gives €371. On P/E, the median of 18.6x on our 2026E EPS gives €251 and the upper quartile €320. Because UCB's growth is in the top half of the group, we use the midpoint of median and upper quartile as the central value: €298 on EV/EBITDA and €285 on P/E.

The model covers the last 44 days of 2026 and the ten years 2027 to 2036, with mid-year discounting from 17 November 2026. In the base case revenue grows 12% in 2027 and slows to 5.5% in 2030, when it reaches €12.8bn. Bimzelx approaches the company's €7bn peak-sales goal in the early 2030s. After 2030 growth falls to 1-3% a year as Cimzia biosimilars arrive, and the margin eases from 41% to 37.5%. Because Bimzelx will lose exclusivity soon after the forecast period, we cut the terminal cash flow by 20% before applying 1.5% growth. Enterprise value is €60.5bn, 56% from the terminal value. After €2.82bn of net debt the equity is worth €57.7bn, or €303 per share. The implied exit multiple is 12.0x EBITDA, below today's peer median. Weighting the scenarios 25/50/25 gives €300.

The discount rate is the main weakness of this model. UCB's 5-year beta of 0.11 is very low because the shares move on trial and sales news, not with the market. Peers are also low (median 0.26). Our stated method gives a cost of equity of 6.6% even with Belgian yields at 4.4%. We keep the method and show the effect: the current price implies a WACC of 6.7%, and each point on the WACC changes the base value by €50 to €75 per share.

Recommendation · Hold · Target €298

Our target is in line with the consensus average of €298.63 from 20 analysts tracked by StockAnalysis, but below the median of €305; 15 of them rate the shares a Buy. We are more cautious than the buyers on two points: we cut the terminal value for Bimzelx's loss of exclusivity, and we assume net prices in the US keep falling slowly. We would turn positive below €260, or if the 2027 guidance in February points to EBITDA growth above 15%.

08Risks and catalysts

Risks

  • Concentration. Bimzelx is 37% of net sales and rising. A safety issue or a strong new rival (oral IL-23 drugs, for example) would hit the whole group.
  • US pricing. The August 2026 agreement with the US government protects current products from Section 232 tariffs, but UCB committed to a "more balanced" pricing approach for future launches.
  • Loss of exclusivity. Briviact generics in 2026, Cimzia biosimilars after 2030, Bimzelx in the next decade.
  • Currency. Most growth is in dollars; a weaker dollar cut reported growth by several points in 2026.
  • ESG. UCB scores 86/100 on our ESG method (Leader), thanks to an MSCI AA rating, a low Sustainalytics risk of 13.7 and a CDP Climate A; we found no recent controversy.

Catalysts

  • FY2026 results and 2027 guidance, 25 February 2027 (confirmed by the company).
  • Sale of the priority review voucher, which could add a few points to 2026 growth.
  • STACCATO alprazolam Phase 3 results, expected between Q4 2026 and H1 2027.
  • Bimzelx paediatric HS results in H1 2027 and the rozanolixizumab MOGAD study in H2 2027.
  • Candid integration: cizutamig Phase 2 studies planned to start by the end of 2026.
