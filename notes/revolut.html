01Thesis and valuation view

We rate Revolut's latest private valuation as STRETCHED: our fair value is $72bn, 40% below the $121.2bn implied by secondary trading in October 2026. Revolut is one of the best-run fintechs in the world: revenue grew 46% in 2025 to $6.0bn with a 38% pre-tax margin. But $121bn is 51 times our 2026 earnings estimate, about 2.5 times the multiple of listed peers.

Three points

  • Growth with profits. 2025 was the fifth profitable year in a row. Eleven product lines each make more than £100m, so the business no longer depends on one source of income.
  • The UK banking licence changes the model. Full bank status lets Revolut lend customer deposits, which should raise interest income, but also brings bank-level capital rules.
  • The price is set by a thin market. The $121bn figure comes from employee share sales marked by Forge, not from a new primary round. Only a small number of shares change hands at that price.
What would make us wrong

If Revolut keeps growing above 30% a year to 2031 and lists in New York at 30x earnings, our bull case gives $134bn today. US-listed Robinhood trades at 37x forward earnings, so a premium listing is possible.

02The surprise: $45bn to $121bn in two years

Revolut's value has risen faster than its profits. In August 2024 a share sale valued it at $45bn. In November 2025 another sale set $75bn. In July 2026 a secondary sale confirmed $115bn, and on 15 October 2026 Forge Global marked the shares at $2,125 each, or about $121.2bn. That makes Revolut more valuable than BNP Paribas, Europe's largest bank by assets (about $108bn). The CEO says an IPO is about two years away, and the Financial Times reports an internal target of $150-200bn.

The mix is broad. Card fees (about $1.0bn), interest income (about $1.0bn), wealth and trading ($0.9bn), FX ($0.8bn) and subscriptions ($0.7bn, +67%) are all large. Subscriptions are the most valuable line because they recur every month and do not depend on interest rates.

Customer balances rose 66% to $67.5bn, and transaction volumes rose 65% to £1.3 trillion. Revenue per customer is still low, which is the main upside if Revolut sells more lending and wealth products to existing users.

06Main model: earnings at exit

Verdict · Stretched · Fair value $72bn

Our fair value is close to the November 2025 valuation of $75bn. Since then the price has risen 62% while the business has grown at about 40% a year. We would see $121bn as supported if Revolut showed 2026 pre-tax profit above $3.5bn and a clear path to US banking profitability.

08Risks and catalysts

Risks

  • Regulation: bank status brings capital requirements that could slow growth.
  • Rates: lower rates reduce interest income on $67bn of balances.
  • US expansion: licences pending; the US is a crowded market.
  • Liquidity of shares: secondary prices can fall quickly.

Catalysts

  • 2026 annual report (expected spring 2027).
  • US banking licence decision.
  • £10bn five-year investment plan and new markets such as Mexico.
  • IPO preparation, expected in about two years.
